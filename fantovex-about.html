Where we started

Fantovex began as a small, self-built project — no big budget, no sponsors, just a genuine love for competitive Free Fire and BGMI. Every tournament, every registration form, every rule was built from scratch by someone who plays the games themselves and knows what a good tournament experience should feel like.

That's still the core of it today. As we grow, the goal stays the same: run tournaments players actually want to come back for, and build a community around fair, exciting competition — not just for the biggest names, but for anyone ready to play beyond their limits.
